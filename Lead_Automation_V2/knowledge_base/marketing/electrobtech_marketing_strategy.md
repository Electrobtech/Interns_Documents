# Electrobtech Marketing Strategy & Course Offerings

## Page 1: Company Overview and Market Positioning

Electrobtech is a leading technology education company operating through two primary wings: the Projects Division and the Academic Division. Our mission is to bridge the gap between theoretical education and practical industry requirements by offering comprehensive training programs in emerging technologies.

### Core Business Divisions

**Projects Division:** Focuses on real-world implementation projects, corporate training, and industry partnerships. This division works directly with companies to provide customized training solutions, project development services, and technology consulting.

**Academic Division:** Dedicated to individual skill development through structured courses, internships, and workshops. This division serves students, professionals, and career changers seeking to enhance their technical capabilities.

### Target Market Segments

Our primary target audiences include:
- Engineering students (B.Tech/B.E/M.Tech) seeking practical skills
- Working professionals looking for career advancement
- Corporate clients requiring employee upskilling
- Educational institutions seeking partnership opportunities
- Government organizations requiring technical training

### Competitive Advantages

Electrobtech distinguishes itself through:
1. Industry-aligned curriculum developed by practicing professionals
2. Hands-on project-based learning methodology
3. Strong placement support and industry connections
4. Flexible learning formats (online, offline, hybrid)
5. Affordable pricing with scholarship opportunities
6. Certification programs recognized by leading companies

## Page 2: Course Portfolio and Marketing Channels

### Comprehensive Course Offerings

**Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning (AIML):**
- Duration: 12-16 weeks
- Coverage: Python programming, neural networks, deep learning, computer vision, NLP
- Projects: Real-world AI applications, predictive models, automation systems
- Target audience: Software developers, data analysts, engineering students

**Data Science:**
- Duration: 10-14 weeks
- Coverage: Statistical analysis, data visualization, machine learning, big data technologies
- Projects: Data analytics dashboards, predictive models, business intelligence solutions
- Target audience: Business analysts, statisticians, IT professionals

**Full Stack Development:**
- Duration: 16-20 weeks
- Coverage: Frontend (React, Angular, Vue), Backend (Node.js, Python, Java), Database management, DevOps basics
- Projects: Complete web applications, e-commerce platforms, SaaS products
- Target audience: Web developers, software engineers, startup founders

**Embedded Systems:**
- Duration: 14-18 weeks
- Coverage: Microcontrollers, IoT development, PCB design, firmware development
- Projects: Smart devices, automation systems, industrial controllers
- Target audience: Electronics engineers, hardware developers, IoT enthusiasts

## Page 3: Pricing Strategy and Customer Acquisition

### Competitive Pricing Structure

**Individual Courses:**
- AIML: ₹25,000 - ₹35,000 depending on format and duration
- Data Science: ₹20,000 - ₹30,000
- Full Stack: ₹30,000 - ₹40,000
- Embedded Systems: ₹28,000 - ₹38,000

**Bundle Packages:**
- Two-course combo: 15% discount
- Complete tech stack (all courses): 25% discount
- Group enrollment (3+ students): Additional 10% discount

**Corporate Training:**
- Customized programs: ₹50,000 - ₹2,00,000 based on scope and duration
- Volume discounts for multiple batches
- Annual retainer models for ongoing training needs

### Customer Acquisition Funnel

**Awareness Stage:**
- Free introductory workshops and webinars
- Social media content and technical blogs
- Campus presentations and career counseling sessions
- Industry conference participation

**Consideration Stage:**
- Free trial classes and course demos
- Detailed curriculum sharing and syllabus discussions
- Alumni testimonials and success stories
- One-on-one counseling sessions with course advisors

**Conversion Stage:**
- Limited-time early bird discounts
- Flexible payment options (EMI available)
- Money-back guarantee for first week
- Free placement assistance enrollment

**Retention Stage:**
- Alumni network access and job referrals
- Advanced course discounts for existing students
- Continuous learning resources and updates
- Community events and networking opportunities

### Key Performance Indicators

Marketing effectiveness is measured through:
- Lead conversion rates across channels
- Cost per acquisition (CPA)
- Student satisfaction scores and referrals
- Course completion rates
- Placement success percentages
- Customer lifetime value (CLV)
- Brand awareness metrics in target markets
