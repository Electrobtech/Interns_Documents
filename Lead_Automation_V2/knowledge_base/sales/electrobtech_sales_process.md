# Electrobtech Sales Process and Methodology

## Page 1: Sales Framework and Customer Journey

### Sales Organization Structure

Electrobtech operates a dual-focused sales organization serving both B2B (corporate training) and B2C (individual courses) markets. Our sales methodology is designed to address the unique needs of each segment while maintaining consistent quality and customer experience.

**B2B Sales Team (Corporate Training):**
- Enterprise Account Managers: Handle large corporate clients and government organizations
- Mid-Market Specialists: Focus on SMEs and mid-sized companies
- Partnership Managers: Manage educational institution partnerships
- Proposal Writers: Create customized training proposals

**B2C Sales Team (Individual Courses):**
- Course Advisors: Guide students through course selection and enrollment
- Admissions Counselors: Handle scholarship applications and financial aid
- Placement Coordinators: Support career services and job placement
- Customer Success Managers: Ensure student satisfaction and retention

### Customer Journey Mapping

**Awareness Stage:**
- Touchpoints: Social media, website, referrals, events, partnerships
- Key Activities: Content consumption, webinar attendance, demo requests
- Sales Role: Lead qualification and initial engagement
- Conversion Goal: Schedule consultation or demo

**Consideration Stage:**
- Touchpoints: Consultation calls, course demos, curriculum review, testimonials
- Key Activities: Needs assessment, budget discussion, timeline planning
- Sales Role: Needs discovery and solution presentation
- Conversion Goal: Proposal submission or enrollment invitation

**Decision Stage:**
- Touchpoints: Proposal review, negotiation, contract signing, payment processing
- Key Activities: Stakeholder alignment, approval processes, final objections
- Sales Role: Objection handling and closing
- Conversion Goal: Contract signed or enrollment completed

**Onboarding Stage:**
- Touchpoints: Welcome communication, platform access, introductory sessions
- Key Activities: Account setup, orientation, first class attendance
- Sales Role: Handover to delivery team and satisfaction check
- Conversion Goal: Successful program start

### Lead Qualification Framework

**BANT Qualification Criteria:**

**Budget (B):**
- Individual courses: ₹20,000 - ₹40,000 range
- Corporate training: ₹50,000 - ₹5,00,000+ range
- Payment flexibility: EMI options available for individual courses
- Corporate payment: Net 30 terms for established clients

**Authority (A):**
- Individual: Student or professional making personal investment
- Corporate: HR Manager, L&D Head, CTO, or CEO approval required
- Educational: Principal, HOD, or Training Coordinator authority
- Government: Department head with budget allocation

**Need (N):**
- Individual: Career advancement, skill upgrade, job placement
- Corporate: Employee upskilling, technology adoption, project requirements
- Educational: Curriculum enhancement, industry alignment, placement improvement
- Government: Digital transformation, skill development initiatives

**Timeline (T):**
- Individual: Immediate to 3 months for course start
- Corporate: 1-6 months for program planning and delivery
- Educational: Academic calendar alignment (semester-based)
- Government: Fiscal year planning and budget cycles

## Page 2: Sales Techniques and Objection Handling

### Consultative Selling Approach

**Discovery Phase Questions:**

**For Individual Students:**
- "What specific skills are you looking to develop?"
- "How will this course help your career goals?"
- "What's your timeline for making this investment?"
- "Have you considered other training options?"
- "What concerns do you have about online learning?"

**For Corporate Clients:**
- "What business challenges are you trying to solve?"
- "How many employees need this training?"
- "What's your current training methodology?"
- "What ROI are you expecting from this investment?"
- "What's your decision-making process and timeline?"

**For Educational Institutions:**
- "How does this align with your curriculum goals?"
- "What industry partnerships do you currently have?"
- "How will this benefit your students' placement prospects?"
- "What's your budget for external training programs?"
- "When would you like to implement this partnership?"

### Value Proposition Framework

**Individual Course Value:**
- Practical, hands-on learning vs. theoretical education
- Industry-aligned curriculum developed by practitioners
- Real-world projects for portfolio building
- Placement assistance and career support
- Flexible learning schedules (online/offline/hybrid)
- Affordable pricing with EMI options

**Corporate Training Value:**
- Customized curriculum aligned to business needs
- Experienced instructors with industry expertise
- Flexible delivery formats (on-site/online/hybrid)
- Post-training support and consultation
- Measurable learning outcomes and assessments
- Volume discounts and long-term partnership benefits

**Educational Partnership Value:**
- Enhanced curriculum with industry-relevant content
- Faculty development and training programs
- Student placement support and industry connections
- Joint certification programs
- Research and development collaboration opportunities
- Brand enhancement through industry association

### Common Objections and Responses

**Price Objections:**
- "It's too expensive" → Focus on ROI and career impact
- "I can find cheaper alternatives" → Emphasize quality and outcomes
- "Can you offer a discount?" → Explain value and available payment options
- "Budget constraints" → Offer EMI,分期付款, or deferred payment

**Timing Objections:**
- "Not the right time" → Understand timeline and follow up appropriately
- "Need to think about it" → Set specific follow-up date and next steps
- "Too busy right now" → Highlight flexible scheduling options
- "Waiting for better offer" → Explain current value and limited availability

**Quality Concerns:**
- "How do I know it's good?" → Share testimonials, success stories, demo content
- "What if I don't like it?" → Explain refund policy and trial options
- "Is this recognized?" → Detail certifications and industry recognition
- "What about placement?" → Share placement statistics and company partnerships

**Competition Objections:**
- "Other providers are cheaper" → Focus on unique value proposition
- "Why choose you over competitors?" → Highlight differentiators and outcomes
- "I'm already considering another option" → Understand decision criteria
- "Can you match their price?" → Explain value differences and alternatives

## Page 3: Sales Performance Management

- Sales cycle length: 2-4 weeks
- Follow-up effectiveness: 60% response rate
- Customer satisfaction score: 4.5/5.0

**Corporate Sales Metrics:**
- Lead conversion rate: Target 15-20%
- Average deal size Corporate: ₹1,50,000 - ₹3,00,000
- Sales cycle length: 8-16 weeks
- Proposal win rate: 30-40%
- Customer retention rate: 70% repeat business
- Partnership renewal rate: 80%

**Activity Metrics:**
- Daily calls: 30-40 calls per sales representative
- Weekly meetings: 15-20 consultations/demos
- Monthly proposals: 8-12 proposals submitted
- Quarterly targets: Individual ₹15-20 lakhs, Corporate ₹50-80 lakhs

### Sales Pipeline Management

**Pipeline Stages:**

**Stage 1: Lead Qualification (0-10% probability)**
- Initial contact made
- Basic qualification completed
- Next step: Discovery call scheduled

**Stage 2: Needs Assessment (10-30% probability)**
- Discovery call completed
- Requirements documented
- Next step: Solution presentation

**Stage 3: Proposal Development (30-50% probability)**
- Solution presented
- Proposal submitted
- Next step: Negotiation/decision

**Stage 4: Negotiation (50-75% probability)**
- Terms discussed
- Objections addressed
- Next step: Final approval

**Stage 5: Closing (75-90% probability)**
- Contract review
- Final negotiations
- Next step: Signature/payment

**Stage 6: Won (100% probability)**
- Contract signed
- Payment received
- Handover to delivery team
