## Page 3: Pricing Strategy and Terms

### Pricing Philosophy

Electrobtech's pricing strategy is built on three core principles:

**Value-Based Pricing:**
- Prices reflect the quality and outcomes delivered
- Focus on ROI for students and companies
- Premium pricing for premium outcomes
- Transparent pricing with no hidden costs

**Competitive Positioning:**
- Mid-market positioning between premium and budget providers
- Better quality than budget competitors at comparable prices
- More affordable than premium providers with similar outcomes
- Regular competitive analysis and price adjustments

**Accessibility Focus:**
- Multiple payment options to reduce financial barriers
- Scholarship programs for deserving candidates
- Group discounts to encourage peer learning
- Corporate sponsorship opportunities

### Terms and Conditions

**Payment Terms for Individual Courses:**
- Full payment: Due before course commencement
- EMI options: Through partner banks with standard terms
- Refund policy: 100% refund within first week, 50% within two weeks
- Course transfer allowed with 10% fee within first month
- Pause and resume option available for valid reasons

**Payment Terms for Corporate Training:**
- Standard terms: 50% advance, 50% on completion
- Large programs: 30% advance, 40% mid-program, 30% on completion
- Retainer models: Quarterly payments in advance
- Government clients: As per government payment terms (up to 90 days)
- Cancellation policy: Full refund if cancelled 2 weeks before start

**Additional Fees:**
- Examination fees: Included in course price
- Certification fees: Included in course price
- Material fees: Included in course price
- Hardware kits: Included for Embedded Systems courses
- Late payment fees: 2% per month on overdue amounts
- Rescheduling fees: ₹2,000 per reschedule request

### Special Offers and Promotions

**Seasonal Promotions:**
- New Year Special (January): 15% discount on all courses
- Summer Internship Program (April-June): Special pricing with placement focus
- Back to Academic Year (July-August): 10% discount for students
- Festival Offers (Diwali, etc.): Limited-time discounts and bundles
- Year-End Skill Upgrade (November-December): 20% discount

**Referral Program:**
- Student referral: ₹2,000 discount for referrer, ₹1,000 for referee
- Corporate referral: 5% commission on first contract value
- Educational institution referral: Special partnership benefits

**Early Bird Discounts:**
- 30 days early: 10% discount
- 15 days early: 5% discount
- Limited to first 20 enrollments per batch

### Price Adjustment Policy

**Regular Price Reviews:**
- Annual price review in January
- Based on inflation, market conditions, and value additions
- Existing students protected from price increases
- 30-day notice for any price changes

**Custom Pricing Approval:**
- Discounts above standard levels require management approval
- Custom pricing for large deals requires business case justification
- Strategic pricing for competitive situations requires sales director approval

**Price Match Guarantee:**
- Will match competitor pricing for comparable programs
- Requires proof of competitor offering
- Must meet quality and outcome criteria
- Limited to one price match per customer
