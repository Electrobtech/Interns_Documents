# Electrobtech Customer Service Procedures and Standards

## Page 1: Customer Service Framework

### Service Mission and Values

**Mission:** To provide exceptional, timely, and effective support that ensures every student and client achieves their learning and business objectives with Electrobtech programs.

**Core Service Values:**
- **Responsiveness:** Quick acknowledgment and resolution of all inquiries
- **Empathy:** Understanding and addressing customer concerns with care
- **Expertise:** Providing accurate, knowledgeable solutions
- **Proactivity:** Anticipating needs and preventing issues before they arise
- **Continuous Improvement:** Learning from every interaction to enhance service quality

### Service Level Agreements (SLAs)

**Response Time Standards:**

**Email Inquiries:**
- General inquiries: Within 24 hours
- Technical issues: Within 12 hours
- Urgent matters: Within 4 hours
- Payment and billing: Within 8 hours

**Phone Support:**
- Business hours (9 AM - 6 PM IST): Immediate response
- After hours: Voicemail with next-day callback
- Emergency technical issues: 24/7 hotline for enrolled students

**Live Chat:**
- Available during business hours
- Initial response: Under 2 minutes
- Resolution time: Under 15 minutes for common issues

**Social Media:**
- Public mentions: Within 4 hours
- Direct messages: Within 2 hours during business hours
- Negative comments: Within 1 hour with appropriate response

### Customer Service Organization Structure

**Tier 1 Support (First Line):**
- General information and basic troubleshooting
- Account access and login issues
- Course information and enrollment guidance
- Payment and billing inquiries
- Scheduling and coordination
- Resolution target: 80% of issues at this level

**Tier 2 Support (Specialized):**
- Technical platform issues
- Curriculum and content questions
- Project and assignment guidance
- Advanced troubleshooting
- Escalation from Tier 1
- Resolution target: 15% of issues at this level

**Tier 3 Support (Expert):**
- Complex technical problems
- System integration issues
- Custom solution development
- Corporate client dedicated support
- Escalation from Tier 2
- Resolution target: 5% of issues at this level

**Specialized Support Teams:**
- **Student Success Team:** Academic guidance, progress monitoring, career counseling
- **Technical Support Team:** Platform issues, software problems, hardware troubleshooting
- **Corporate Support Team:** Dedicated account management for corporate clients
- **Placement Support Team:** Job placement assistance, interview preparation, career guidance

### Communication Channels and Protocols

**Primary Communication Channels:**

**Email Support:**
- Dedicated email addresses for different query types
- Ticket system for tracking and resolution
- Automated acknowledgment with ticket number
- Status updates at regular intervals
- Closure confirmation and satisfaction survey

**Phone Support:**
- Toll-free number for enrolled students
- Local numbers for general inquiries
- Callback scheduling for peak times
- Call recording for quality assurance
- Follow-up email summary of conversations

**Live Chat:**
- Website-integrated chat widget
- Mobile app chat functionality
- Chatbot for common queries with human escalation
- Chat transcript availability
- Multi-language support capability

**Self-Service Portal:**
- Comprehensive knowledge base
- FAQ section organized by topic
- Video tutorials and guides
- Community forum for peer support
- Ticket submission and tracking

## Page 2: Issue Resolution Procedures

### Issue Classification and Triage

**Issue Categories:**

**Technical Issues:**
- Platform access and login problems
- Video playback and streaming issues
- Software installation and configuration
- Hardware compatibility problems
- Network connectivity issues
- Priority: High for enrolled students, Medium for prospects

**Academic Issues:**
- Course content clarification
- Assignment and project guidance
- Progress and performance concerns
- Certification and assessment questions
- Curriculum feedback
- Priority: Medium for all students

**Administrative Issues:**
- Enrollment and registration
- Payment and billing
- Schedule and batch changes
- Certificate requests
- Account management
- Priority: Medium for all users

**Placement and Career Issues:**
- Job placement assistance
- Interview preparation
- Resume building support
- Career guidance
- Alumni networking
- Priority: High for final-year students

**Corporate Client Issues:**
- Training program coordination
- Customization requests
- Reporting and analytics
- Account management
- Priority: High with dedicated support

### Issue Resolution Workflow

**Standard Resolution Process:**

1. **Issue Receipt and Acknowledgment:**
   - Automatic ticket creation
   - Immediate acknowledgment to customer
   - Issue classification and priority assignment
   - Routing to appropriate support tier

2. **Initial Assessment:**
   - Gather detailed information from customer
   - Reproduce issue if possible
   - Check knowledge base for known solutions
   - Determine resolution approach

3. **Resolution Implementation:**
   - Apply standard solutions for common issues
   - Escalate complex issues to appropriate specialists
   - Keep customer informed of progress
   - Document all actions taken

4. **Verification and Closure:**
   - Confirm resolution with customer
   - Test solution effectiveness
   - Update knowledge base with new solutions
   - Close ticket with satisfaction survey

**Escalation Procedures:**

**Tier 1 to Tier 2 Escalation:**
- Issue not resolved within 24 hours
- Requires specialized technical knowledge
- Customer requests escalation
- Multiple attempts to resolve failed

**Tier 2 to Tier 3 Escalation:**
- Issue not resolved within 48 hours
- Requires expert-level intervention
- System-wide or platform-level problems
- Critical business impact for corporate clients

**Management Escalation:**
- Customer dissatisfaction with resolution
- Repeated issues with same customer
- Service level agreement breaches
- High-value client concerns

### Common Issue Resolution Templates

**Login and Access Issues:**
- Verify account status and payment
- Check for system-wide outages
- Guide through password reset process
- Clear browser cache and cookies
- Verify compatible browser and device
- Create temporary access if needed

**Video Playback Issues:**
- Check internet connection speed
- Verify browser compatibility
- Update video player software
- Provide alternative video quality options
- Offer downloadable content when available
- Schedule live session as alternative

**Payment and Billing Issues:**
- Verify payment transaction status
- Check for failed transactions
- Provide payment receipt copies
- Process refunds according to policy
- Set up payment plans if eligible
- Escalate to finance team for complex issues

**Course Content Questions:**
- Refer to specific curriculum sections
- Provide additional examples and explanations
- Schedule instructor consultation if needed
- Connect with peers for discussion
- Provide supplementary resources
- Record question for curriculum review
