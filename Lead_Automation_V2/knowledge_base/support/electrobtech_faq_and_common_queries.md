# Electrobtech FAQ and Common Customer Queries

## Page 1: General Course and Enrollment FAQs

### Course-Related Questions

**Q: What are the prerequisites for enrolling in Electrobtech courses?**
A: Prerequisites vary by course:
- **AIML:** Basic programming knowledge (Python preferred), understanding of mathematics (statistics, linear algebra)
- **Data Science:** Programming basics, statistical mathematics, analytical thinking
- **Full Stack:** Basic HTML/CSS knowledge, logical reasoning, problem-solving skills
- **Embedded Systems:** Electronics fundamentals, C/C++ programming, basic circuit knowledge

**Q: Can I enroll if I have no prior programming experience?**
A: Yes! We offer foundation courses for beginners. For complete beginners, we recommend starting with our programming fundamentals course before advancing to specialized programs. Our instructors provide additional support for students with limited prior experience.

**Q: What is the duration of each course?**
A: Course durations vary:
- **Basic Programs:** 10-16 weeks (self-paced)
- **Standard Programs:** 14-20 weeks (live classes)
- **Premium Programs:** 18-24 weeks (hybrid with extensive support)

**Q: What is the difference between Basic, Standard, and Premium programs?**
A: The main differences are:
- **Basic:** Self-paced learning, video content, basic support, fewer projects
- **Standard:** Live instructor-led sessions, mentorship, more projects, placement assistance
- **Premium:** Small batches, 1-on-1 counseling, advanced projects, placement guarantee, interview prep

**Q: Can I switch between program types after enrollment?**
A: Yes, you can upgrade from Basic to Standard or Premium within the first 2 weeks. Downgrades are not permitted. Upgrades require payment of the price difference.

### Schedule and Format Questions

**Q: What are the available batch timings?**
A: We offer flexible scheduling:
- **Weekend Batches:** Saturday-Sunday (9 AM - 1 PM or 2 PM - 6 PM)
- **Weekday Batches:** Monday-Friday (7 AM - 9 AM or 7 PM - 9 PM)
- **Self-Paced:** Learn at your own schedule with recorded content
- **Corporate Batches:** Customized timing based on company requirements

**Q: Can I change my batch timing?**
A: Batch changes are permitted within the first week subject to availability. A rescheduling fee of ₹2,000 applies for changes requested after the first week.

**Q: What if I miss a live class?**
A: Missed classes are recorded and available for viewing. You can also:
- Attend makeup sessions scheduled monthly
- Request doubt-clearing sessions for missed topics
- Access supplementary materials and assignments
- Coordinate with peers for notes and discussions

**Q: Is the program completely online or are there offline components?**
A: We offer multiple formats:
- **Online Only:** 100% remote learning
- **Hybrid:** Online classes with optional offline workshops
- **Offline:** Classroom-based learning at select locations
- Premium programs include both online and offline components
